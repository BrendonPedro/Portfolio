Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm Brendon Pedro — an AI engineer and full-stack developer from Cape Town, South Africa, now based in Taiwan, where I lead engineering at an IP-licensing startup while completing a master's in Electrical & Computer Engineering at NYCU. The short version: I build production AI platforms end to end. The longer version is a better story.

I grew up in a close-knit, lower-middle-class family of four that treats education as a team sport. I worked my way through the University of the Western Cape as a top telesales agent, and spent five consecutive years as a senior marker for longitudinal testing with the Schools Development Unit at the University of Cape Town — self-funding a B.A. in Anthropology, Linguistics and Psychology, an Advanced Diploma in Management, and a B.Com. (Honours) in Management specialising in entrepreneurial learning.

I put that entrepreneurship to work immediately. I founded Linguistically Cultured, a custom clothing and digital printing business that also managed hip-hop artists, and ran it for four years — along the way winning a government grant that provided our e-commerce website and various subsidies. In parallel, I spent nearly three years at Digital Outsource Services (DigiOutsource, part of Super Group), a global technology company in online gaming, as a Service Lead managing three teams through customer operations, problem resolution, and the advanced software systems behind them. Those years taught me how to coach, facilitate, and manage people — skills no compiler can teach you.

In 2018, my partner and I — we met back in South Africa in 2009 — moved to Taiwan together. I taught English in Zhubei (I still teach part-time — the educator in me never left) and studied Chinese at NCTU for two semesters. I had also set my sights on the MBA my father never got the chance to finish before he passed away in 2020. In 2023, I completed the International MBA at National Tsing Hua University with distinctions across my modules — finishing what he started (the IMBA programme even featured my story in their Student Voices). Taiwan gave us more than degrees, too: in 2022, thirteen years after we first met, I married my wife here.

The MBA pulled me toward data and code. What began as evening courses in SQL and Python became a full engineering career: in 2024 I founded CEMTA, an AI menu-translation platform that turns Traditional Chinese menus into English and Pinyin with OCR and Vertex AI. Since June 2026 I've been the AI & Engineering Lead — and sole engineer — at Pork Belly Creative, where I designed, built, and shipped Cortex, the company's production IP & outreach platform (Next.js, TypeScript, Clerk, Supabase Postgres with row-level security, Vercel), plus an AI fit-assessment engine on the Claude API with versioned output contracts, an MCP server with propose-then-human-review writes, and PII tokenization for Taiwan PDPA compliance. I'm doing this while pursuing my M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering at NYCU with a Computer Science and AI focus.

I'm a vibrant, career-driven person who genuinely enjoys working with people. Whether I'm shipping a platform solo, coaching a team, or standing in front of a classroom, the goal is the same: keep learning, build things that matter, and bring others along. I'd love the chance to do that with you.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
