About Me

I'm a vibrant, life-long learner who enjoys working with people. My deep passion for learning led me to becoming an entrepreneur and educator while pursuing an education in business, data analytics, and developing cool apps.

That streak started back in South Africa with Linguistically Cultured, my first company (custom clothing & digital printing, 2014–2018) — and the educator in me never left: I've taught ESL English in Taiwan since 2018, and still do.

These days I build production AI platforms end to end — full-stack web apps, LLM-powered engines, and agentic pipelines — with TypeScript, React/Next.js, Python, PostgreSQL, and the Claude API.

I believe in training both body and mind. You'll find me lifting weights, sparring in the ring, or strategizing over a chessboard with equal enthusiasm.

Cape Town born and raised, from a close-knit family of four who back each other in life and academics. Best unlock so far: I met my wife in 2020 and married her in 2022 — we're building our life together in Taiwan.
